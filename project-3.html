Project Overview

Employee Survey is an internal feedback platform focusing on delivering a smooth and reliable experience for both employees and administrators. The project aimed to simplify the process of collecting, tracking, and analyzing employee feedback within an organization. The platform was crafted to be a visually appealing and functional tool that effectively captures employee sentiment and insights. The creation of a clean, intuitive interface allows employees to easily submit responses, while administrators can view and manage survey data with ease. The Employee Survey platform was fully responsive, providing seamless accessibility across various devices.

The platform has received positive feedback, with users reporting high satisfaction with its simplicity and ease of use. The improved functionality and responsive design have contributed to better employee participation and more effective data collection for organizational decision-making.
